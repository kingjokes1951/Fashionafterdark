The Production Advantage

Present your work inside a produced environment.

Producing a show independently means sourcing models, coordinating beauty teams, building a run of show, securing media and managing the room. Our productions consolidate those elements so designers can focus on the collection and the presentation.

Produced in connection with Higher Frequencies Health and Human Development Foundation and HAUS of CHAINZ, we bridge the gap between emerging talent and industry standard production.

Sponsored Designer Consideration

Sponsored Designer status is NOT a package that can be automatically purchased. This designation is reserved for select, elite, prestigious, established, nationally or internationally recognized designers and fashion brands whose professional reputation, collection quality, industry presence, brand positioning, body of work, or influence aligns with the caliber of the production.

Sponsored Designations May Be Awarded Based On:

  • Established professional brand or label
  • Strong professional portfolio and body of work
  • High quality, runway-ready collection
  • Recognized industry presence or reputation
  • National or international brand presence
  • Significant press, editorial, celebrity, entertainment, retail, or fashion industry recognition
  • Alignment with production core values and aesthetic requirements
