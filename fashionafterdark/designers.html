Essential Production Resources

What a production may include.

Designer participation and exact deliverables depend on the individual production or package.

01

Model Casting

Casting access to an extensive community of adult and youth/teen models.

02

Runway Production

Show run down, staging, run of show and production coordination.

03

Designer Coordination

Assistance around fittings, lineup, looks and timing.

04

Event Ambassadors

Trained support roles assisting guests, backstage flow and designer needs.

05

Photography

Runway and event coverage circulated by the production media team.

06

Videography

Video native content suitable for brand and media use.

07

Hair & Makeup Teams

Beauty teams working to the direction of the production or collection.

08

Styling Support

Wardrobe and styling assistants for presentation cohesiveness.

09

Red Carpet Opportunities

Artisanal dress adornments available in select collections.

* Features depend on exact scope of agreement based on location, facility guidelines and budget limitations. List is not exhaustive and is variable.

Sponsored Consideration

Sponsored Designer status is NOT a package that can be automatically purchased. This designation is reserved for select, elite, internationally recognized designers whose reputation aligns with the caliber of the production.

Designations Awarded Based On:

  • Established professional brand or label
  • Strong professional portfolio and body of work
  • High quality, runway-ready collection
  • Recognized industry presence or reputation
  • Significant press, editorial, or celebrity recognition
  • Alignment with production core values
