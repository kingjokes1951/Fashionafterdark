Scouting & Representation

Become a Face.

We are currently scouting for our New Faces board as well as reviewing established talent for representation. Please complete the digital application below for consideration.

Submission Guidelines

Photographs

We require raw, unedited polaroids. Do not submit professional photos, selfies with filters, or heavily retouched images. Natural lighting is best.

Attire for Photos

Wear form-fitting, solid color clothing. Black or white is preferred. Skinny jeans and a plain tank top work perfectly.

Hair & Makeup

Hair should be pulled away from the face. Absolutely no makeup. We need to see your natural bone structure and skin.

Response Time

Due to the high volume of submissions, our scouting team will only reach out if there is interest in setting up an interview. Typical response time is 2-3 weeks.
